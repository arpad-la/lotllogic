How do I play?

Place one axolotl in every coloured pond, one in every row and one in every column. Axolotls never touch, not even diagonally. Use Mark to drop pebbles on spots that can't hold an axolotl, then Place to put one down. The rules are also under the ? button in every level and in the Field Guide on the Collection tab.

Why does placing take two taps?

The first tap shows where the axolotl would go and the second places it, so a stray tap never costs a gill. You can switch to one tap in Settings → Place an axolotl with.

How do hints and Tide Sweeps work?

A hint highlights the next logical step and explains why it works. A Tide Sweep pebbles every spot that is already ruled out. You start with 3 of each and get one more of each every day you play.

I bought Remove Ads. How do I get it on another device?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases. The purchase works on all your devices.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, open Settings → Ad privacy choices. Anywhere, you can turn off tracking for all apps in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

How do I stop the daily reminder?

Turn off Settings → Daily reminder in the game, or turn off notifications for Axodokutl in iOS Settings. The reminder only arrives on days you haven't opened the game.

My progress is gone

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes it, and Settings → Reset progress erases it on purpose. Game Center keeps your leaderboard scores and achievements.

Game Center isn't showing

Sign in to Game Center in iOS Settings → Game Center, then reopen the game. The Game Center row appears in Settings once you're signed in.
