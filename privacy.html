Axodokutl Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Axodokutl is a puzzle game for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. We don't run our own servers, and we never sell your personal data.

Your progress stays with you

Your levels, scores, pearls, streaks and settings are saved on your device. We can't see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

If you turn on Save progress in iCloud in Settings, a copy of your progress is also kept in your own iCloud account, so it stays the same on your other devices. Apple stores it under the Apple Privacy Policy; we can't see it. Turn the setting off to stop syncing.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app shows ads between levels. The ads come from Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • device information, such as model, operating system version and language;
  • your approximate location, based on your IP address;
  • the advertising identifier (IDFA), but only if you allow tracking when the app asks;
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps.

If you don't allow tracking, you still get ads, but they are less personalized. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice later in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Google's use of this data is covered by How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can also turn off tracking for all apps in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Remove Ads purchase

You can remove all ads with a one-time purchase. Apple handles the payment through the App Store. We never see your name, card or payment details; we only learn whether the purchase is owned so we can turn ads off.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, the app sends your scores and achievements to Apple's Game Center so they appear on leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. You can sign out of Game Center in iOS Settings.

Children

Axodokutl is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
